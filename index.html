The Exponential Growth of Christianity (~30–100 AD)

The New Testament records one of the most astonishing transformations in human history: the exponential multiplication of a humble gathering in Roman Judea into a vibrant spiritual movement spanning the entire Mediterranean world within a single generation.

Four Waves of Apostolic Expansion

  • Wave 1 — Pentecost & Judea (30–34 AD): Endowed with the Holy Ghost, apostles bore eyewitness testimony of Christ's resurrection.
  • Wave 2 — Samaria & Syrian Antioch (34–44 AD): Scattered by persecution, disciples shared the Word beyond Jewish borders; Gentiles poured in at Antioch.
  • Wave 3 — Paul's Missionary Journeys (47–62 AD): Crossing 10,000+ miles via Roman roads and seas, Paul planted assemblies in Galatia, Macedonia, Greece, and Asia.
  • Wave 4 — Apostolic Consolidation (62–100 AD): Surviving imperial persecutions and Jerusalem's 70 AD fall, the Church matured into resilient regional networks across three continents.
